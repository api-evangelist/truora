Identity verification is the process of establishing enough confidence that a person is who they say they are before a business opens an account, grants access, approves a transaction, or allows another sensitive action. It is not a ceremonial document upload. In a digital journey, the company is making a decision with incomplete information, often in seconds, and the cost of getting it wrong can appear much later as fraud, support tickets, losses, or a weak customer record.
