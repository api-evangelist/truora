What is K-ID in identity verification? K-ID (Know Your Identity) refers to advanced identity verification processes that combine multiple technologies to validate a user’s identity in digital environments. It goes beyond traditional KYC (Know Your Customer) by incorporating real-time data validation, biometrics, and AI-driven analysis.
